Privacy Policy

Sai Keerthana & Michael Wedding RSVP · operated by Maha Yadavalli · Last updated 25 September 2026

This policy explains what information Maha Yadavalli ("we") collects through the wedding invitation website at https://maharanay22.github.io/sai-michael-wedding/ and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number.
  • Whether you will attend, and how many adults and children are coming.
  • An optional note or blessing for the couple.
  • Whether you ticked the box to receive text messages (your SMS opt-in).

How we use it

  • To plan the wedding of Sai Keerthana and Michael on 6 November 2026 (guest count, seating and catering).
  • If you opted in: to text you one RSVP confirmation and one reminder the day before the wedding.

Sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates. We never sell, rent or share your mobile number or SMS consent with anyone.

Your RSVP is stored in Google Firebase, and texts are delivered through Twilio. These are technical service providers acting only on our instructions to run this RSVP service; they do not receive your information for their own use, marketing or promotional purposes.

Storage and deletion

RSVPs are stored in Google Firebase and can be read only by the hosts. We delete all RSVP information within 60 days after the wedding. To have your information deleted sooner, contact maharanay@gmail.com.

Text messages (SMS)

If you tick the optional "Text me" box on the RSVP form, you agree to receive up to 3 text messages from Maha Yadavalli about the Sai Keerthana & Michael wedding: an RSVP confirmation and a reminder the day before. Consent is not required to RSVP. Message frequency: up to 3 messages in total. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help, or call or text (409) 273-9225. See our Terms & Conditions.
