RSVPs are stored in Google Firebase and can be read only by the hosts. We delete all RSVP information within 60 days after the wedding. To have your information deleted sooner, contact maharanay@gmail.com.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions.
