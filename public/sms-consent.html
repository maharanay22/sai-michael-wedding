SMS Opt-in (Text Message Consent)

Sai Keerthana & Michael Wedding RSVP texts · operated by Maha Yadavalli

How guests opt in

  1. A guest opens the wedding invitation website: https://maharanay22.github.io/sai-michael-wedding/
  2. They scroll to the RSVP form (#rsvp) and enter their name, mobile number and whether they will attend.
  3. Below the mobile number is an optional checkbox that is unticked by default. Texts are sent only if the guest ticks it. Guests can RSVP without ticking it.
